Our Roots

NATIVE MOUNTAIN GUIDES SINCE 2018

Golo Holidays started as a cooperative of native Ladakhi guides in Leh who wanted to offer structured, professional, and safe travel support for visitors exploring the delicate environment of Ladakh.

Unlike online booking aggregators, we own our fleet of vehicles, manage direct custom agreements with verified local homestays, and train all our guides in high-altitude safety. Over the years, we expanded our operations to the cold deserts of Spiti Valley and the green alpine meadows of Kashmir, keeping our local-first core philosophy intact.
